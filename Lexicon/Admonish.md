# Admonish

ədˈmɒn.ɪʃ

- to tell someone that they have done something wrong
- to advise someone to do something
- to warn someone not to do something, usually in a kind way, or to tell someone to do something

Similar words: [[Reprimand]], [[Advise]]

Mnemonic: Advise Monish about his bad behavior

Example: His mother admonished him for eating too quickly.
