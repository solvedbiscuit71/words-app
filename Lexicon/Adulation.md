# Adulation

#hard 

/ˌædʒ.əˈleɪ.ʃən/

- very great admiration or praise for someone, especially when it is more than is deserved
- too much admiration or praise for someone

Similar words: [[Adoration]], [[Praise]]

Mnemonic: In order to be add + u + later, you need to first adulation someone.

Example: He couldn’t deal with the adulation of his fans.
