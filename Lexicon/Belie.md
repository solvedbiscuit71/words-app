# Belie

#hard 

bɪˈlaɪ

- to show something to be false, or to hide something such as an emotion
- to represent something falsely or to hide something

Similar words: [[Contradict]], [[Disproving]]

Mnemonic: (1) Being in lie state i.e. to hide something (2) show something is belied.
