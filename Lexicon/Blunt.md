# Blunt

/blʌnt/

- having an edge or point that is not sharp
- saying what you think without trying to be polite or considering other people's feelings
- to make something less sharp or a feeling less strong

Similar words: [[Abrupt]], [[Dull]]

Mnemonic: A blunt pencil is an indicator of a blunt mind

Example: I'll be blunt - that last piece of work you did was terrible.
