# Caricature

ˈkær.ɪ.kə.tʃʊər

- a drawing that makes someone look funny or foolish because some part of the person's appearance is exaggerated
- someone or something that is very exaggerated in a funny or foolish way

Similar words: [[Parody]], [[Satire]], [[Exaggeration]]

Mnemonic: Jimmy carry is famous for his facial caricature.

Example: Over the years he's become a grotesque caricature of himself
