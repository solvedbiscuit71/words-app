# Charismatic

char·​is·​mat·​ic

- a special charm or appeal that causes people to feel attracted and excited by someone
- a special quality that some people have naturally that makes them able to influence other people and attract their attention and admiration
- the ability to attract the attention and admiration of others, and to be seen as a leader
