# Concede

con·​cede

- to say that you accept or do not deny the truth or existence of (something) in an unwilling way
- to admit that you have been defeated and stop trying to win
- to give away (something) usually in an unwilling way
- to allow an opponent to score (a point, goal, etc.)

Similar words: [[Admit]], [[Succumb]]

Mnemonic: When you're wrong, just concede and move along.
