# Conciliation

kənˌsɪl.iˈeɪ.ʃən

- the action or process of ending a disagreement, often by discussion between the groups or people involved
- the process of helping two sides in a disagreement to meet and talk about their different ideas in the hope of ending the disagreement

Similar words: [[Reconcile]]

Mnemonic: Conciliation, reconciliation, means the same.

Example: All attempts at conciliation failed and the dispute continued.
