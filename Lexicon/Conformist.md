# Conformist

kənˈfɔː.mɪst

- someone who behaves or thinks like everyone else, rather than being different
- someone who behaves or thinks like the other people in a group and doesn't do anything unusual

Similar words: [[Follower]], [[Docile]]

Mnemonic: One who always conform to the group, is a conformist.

Example: She was the conformist daughter while her sister was a rebel.
