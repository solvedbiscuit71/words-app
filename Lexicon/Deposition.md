# Deposition

de·​po·​si·​tion

- out-of-court testimony that is made under oath by a party or witness (as an expert) in response to oral or written questions and that is recorded by an authorized officer for later use in court
- an act or process of laying someone or something down or letting something fall
- something deposited
- an act of removing from a position of authority
