# Didactic

daɪˈdæk.tɪk

- intended to teach, especially in a way that is too determined or eager, and often fixed and unwilling to change
- intended to teach, or to improve morals by teaching

Similar words: [[Moralizing]], [[Preachy]]

Mnemonic: Didi + act, didi always tries to teach some moral

Example: Children’s books possess a practical, didactic purpose – to instill a love for reading.
