# Dilapidate

də-ˈla-pə-ˌdā-təd

- decayed, deteriorated, or fallen into partial ruin especially through neglect or misuse
- in very bad condition because of age or lack of care

Similar words: [[Neglected]], [[Degraded]]

Near antonyms: [[Maintained]], [[Improved]]

Mnemonic: I drink a diluted apple juice which is outdated, now my stomach is ruined
