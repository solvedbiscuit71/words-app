# Dizzy

diz·​zy

- feeling that you are turning around in circles and are going to fall even though you are standing still
- mentally or emotionally upset or confused
- very high : sometimes used figuratively

Similar words: [[Giddy]], [[Dazed]], [[Rapid]]

Near antonyms: [[Steady]], [[Clear]], [[Slow]]

Mnemonic: Dizzy, dizzy, head is whizzy!
