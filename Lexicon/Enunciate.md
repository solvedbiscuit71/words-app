# Enunciate

enun·​ci·​ate

- to pronounce words or parts of words clearly : articulate, pronounce
- to make a definite or systematic statement of : announce, proclaim

Similar words: [[Speak]], [[Announce]], [[Say]]

Near antonyms: [[Hesitate]], [[Conceal]], [[Stifle]]

Mnemonic: Enunciate means good pronunciation in communication
