# Equanimity

ek.wəˈnɪm.ə.ti

- a calm mental state, especially after a shock or disappointment or in a difficult situation
- the state of being calm and in control of your emotions, esp. in a difficult situation

Similar words: [[Calm]], [[Patient]], [[Uncomplaining]]

Mnemonic: Equal mind, one which is calm and in control of emotions.

Example: Three years after the tragedy she has only just begun to regain her equanimity.
