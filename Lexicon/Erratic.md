# Erratic

i-ˈra-tik

- having no fixed course : wandering
- characterized by lack of consistency, regularity, or uniformity
- deviating from what is ordinary or standard : eccentric

Similar words: [[Random]], [[Sporadic]], [[Changing]], [[Bizarre]]

Near antonyms: [[Regular]], [[Continuous]], [[Constant]], [[Normal]]

Mnemonic: Erratic, chaotic, i.e. never static
