# Espousal

esˈpaʊ.zəlk

- the act of becoming involved with or supporting an activity or opinion
- a marriage or engagement (= promise to marry):

Similar words: [[Support]], [[Adoption]], [[Betrothal]]

Mnemonic: (1) Engage with Spouse, i.e. become involved an activity (2) literally, marriage

Example: She is known for her espousal of environmental causes.
