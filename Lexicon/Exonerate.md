# Exonerate

ɪɡˈzɒn.ə.reɪt

- to show or state that someone or something is not guilty of something
- to show or state that someone or something is not to be blamed for something bad that happened

Similar words: [[Innocent]], [[Exculpate]]

Mnemonic: Ex(gone) + honor + ate, to bring back lost honor

Example: We have proof which will completely exonerate him.
