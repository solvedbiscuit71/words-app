# Filibuster

fɪl.ɪ.bʌs.tər

- to make a long speech in order to delay or prevent a new law being made
- a long speech that someone makes in order to delay or prevent a new law being made

Similar words: [[Lecturing]], [[Addressing]]

Mnemonic: He filled the day, bastard, with his long speech, so no voting could be made.

Example: He started a filibuster to stop the drafting of young men into military service.
