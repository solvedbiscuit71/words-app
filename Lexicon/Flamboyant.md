# Flamboyant

/flæmˈbɔɪ.ənt/

- very confident in your behavior, and liking to be noticed by other people
- intended to be noticed
- brightly colored and easily noticed

Similar words: [[Prominent]], [[Loud]]

Mnemonic: Flame boy, a boy wearing shirt with flames, trying to seek attention.

Example: His clothes were rather flamboyant for such a serious occasion.
