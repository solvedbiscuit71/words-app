# Furtive

fɜː.tɪv

- (of people) behaving secretly and often dishonestly, or (of actions) done secretly and often dishonestly
- done or acting secretly and quietly to avoid being noticed

Similar words: [[Covert]], [[Clandestine]]

Mnemonic: Furtive, rhymes with fugitive, someone who's avoid being noticed.

Example: He cast a furtive glance in our direction.
