# Genuflect

/ˈdʒen.ju.flekt/

- to bend one or both knees as a sign of respect to God, especially when entering or leaving a Catholic church
- to bend one knee or touch one knee to the floor as a sign of worship or respect

Similar words: [[Fawn]]

Mnemonic: Genuine + flect (to bend in latin)

Example: People were genuflecting in front of the altar.
