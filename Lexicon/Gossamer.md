# Gossamer

#hard 

/ˈɡɒs.ə.mər/

- very delicate and light
- the very thin thread that spiders produce to make webs

Similar words: [[Gauzy]], [[Fluffy]], [[Sheer]]

Mnemonic: Go + summer, in summer we wear dress which are delicate and light.

Example: The gossamer veil seemed to float about the bride as she walked down the aisle
