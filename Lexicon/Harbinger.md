# Harbinger

ˈhɑː.bɪn.dʒər

- someone or something that foreshadows a future event
- a person or thing that shows that something is going to happen soon, especially something bad

Similar words: [[Precursor]], [[Sign]]

Mnemonic: Horrible binger, is a sign for something bad

Example: her father's successful job interview was seen as a harbinger of better times to come
