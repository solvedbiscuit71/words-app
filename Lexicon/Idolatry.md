# Idolatry

aɪˈdɒl.ə.tri

- very great admiration or respect for someone or something, often too great
- the act of praying to a picture or object as part of a religion

Similar words: [[Admiration]], [[Respect]], [[Worship]]

Mnemonic: Idol are respected, admired, in worst cases, worship by their fans.

Example: fans of the team are well known for their idolatry.
