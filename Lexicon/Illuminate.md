# Illuminate

il·​lu·​mi·​nate

- to supply or brighten with light
- to enlighten spiritually or intellectually
- to make clear : elucidate
- to bring to the fore : highlight

Similar words: [[Light]], [[Illustrate]], [[Highlight]], [[Educate]]

Near antonyms: [[Dim]], [[Obscure]], [[Confuse]]

Mnemonic: A light so great, it'll illuminate!
