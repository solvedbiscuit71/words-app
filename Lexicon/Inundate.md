# Inundate

ɪn.ʌn.deɪt

- to cause (someone or something) to receive or take in a large amount of things at the same time : overwhelm
- to cover (something) with a flood of water : flood

Similar words: [[Overwhelm]], [[Flood]]

Near antonyms: [[Drain]]

Mnemonic: I + NUN + DATE, I asked a nun for a date, but I was FLOODED with letters from father Nate.
