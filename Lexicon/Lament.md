# Lament

#hard 

/ləˈment/

- to express sadness and feeling sorry about something
- a song, poem, or other piece of writing that expresses sadness about someone's death

Similar words: [[Mourn]], [[Regret]], [[Whine]]

Mnemonic: Lame + end, we express sadness when the ending is lame.

Example: The whole play can be interpreted as a lament for lost youth.
