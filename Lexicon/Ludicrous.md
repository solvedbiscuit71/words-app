# Ludicrous

#hard 

/ˈluː.dɪ.krəs/

- stupid or unreasonable and deserving to be laughed at
- ridiculous or foolish; unreasonable or unsuitable

Similar words: [[Funny]], [[Ridiculous]]

Mnemonic: Ludicrous, ridiculous similar sounds, similar meaning

Example: the ludicrous sight of their teacher in a Halloween costume
