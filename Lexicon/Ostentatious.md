# Ostentatious

 /ˌɒs.tenˈteɪ.ʃəs/

- too obviously showing your money, possessions, or power, in an attempt to make other people notice and admire you
- intended to attract admiration or notice

Similar words: [[Extravagant]], [[Arrogant]]

Mnemonic: Austin is trying to tempt us with his possessions.

Example: They criticized the ostentatious lifestyle of their leaders.
