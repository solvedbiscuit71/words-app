# Pastoral

pɑː.stər.əl

- A piece of art, writing, or music represents the pleasant and traditional features of the countryside
- used to refer to the part of the work of teachers and priests that involves giving help and advice about personal matters

Similar words: [[Rural]], [[Rustic]], [[Idyll]]

Mnemonic: Past + oral, in the past, RURAL people use language in oral form.

Example: The painting depicts an idyllic pastoral scene of shepherds watching over their grazing sheep.
