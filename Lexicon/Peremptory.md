# Peremptory

#hard 

/pəˈremp.tər.i/

- expecting to be obeyed immediately and without any questions
- having the expectation of immediate and complete obedience, or to be obeyed without explanation

Similar words: [[Authoritarian]], [[Compulsory]], [[Arrogant]], [[Arbitrary]]

Mnemonic: Peremptory, sounds like preempt, expecting to be obeyed.

Example: In his usual peremptory manner, he ordered us all into the conference room.
