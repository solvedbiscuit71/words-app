# Perspicacious

/ˌpɜː.spɪˈkeɪ.ʃəs/

- quick in noticing, understanding, or judging things accurately

Similar words: [[Wise]], [[Sagacious]]

Mnemonic: Your perspective is spectacular, your wise.

Example: His perspicacious grandfather had bought the land as an investment, guessing that there might be gold underground.
