# Predicament

/prɪˈdɪk.ə.mənt/

- an unpleasant situation that is difficult to get out of
- an unpleasant or confusing situation that is difficult to get out of or solve

Similar words: [[Dilemma]]

Mnemonic: unable to predict, it a predicament.

Example: I don't know how we're going to get out of this predicament.
