# Prudent

pru·​dent

- marked by wisdom, judiciousness, or restraint
- having or showing good judgment or restraint
- marked by circumspection : discreet
- provident, frugal

Similar words: [[Cautious]], [[Wise]]

Near antonyms: [[Imprudent]], [[Unwise]]

Mnemonic: Prudent and wise, save before it flies.
