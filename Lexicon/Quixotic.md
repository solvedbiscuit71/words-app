# Quixotic

/kwɪkˈsɒt.ɪk/

- having or showing ideas that are different and unusual but not practical or likely to succeed
- having intentions or ideas that are admirable but not practical

Similar words: [[Impractical]], [[Romantic]]

Mnemonic: Quick + exotic, something that sounds great but impractical.

Example: This is a vast, exciting and some say quixotic project.
