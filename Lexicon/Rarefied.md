# Rarefied

ˈreə.rɪ.faɪd

- understood by only a small group of people : only for people who know about a particular thing
- not having much oxygen because of being high up in the atmosphere

Similar words: [[Buoyant]]

Mnemonic: rare + fied, something that's rare

Example: It's difficult to breathe in the rarefied air near the mountain's peak.
