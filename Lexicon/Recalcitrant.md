# Recalcitrant

rɪˈkæl.sɪ.trənt

- (of a person) unwilling to obey orders or to do what should be done, or (of an animal) refusing to be controlled
- unwilling to do what you are asked or ordered to do, even if it is reasonable

Similar words: [[Adamant]], [[Obstinate]]

Mnemonic: Recalcitrant, rhymes with "cannot be reasoned with", being adamant.

Example: Specific mechanisms associated with desiccation sensitivity of recalcitrant seeds are still not resolved.
