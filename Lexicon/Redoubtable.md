# Redoubtable

/rɪˈdaʊ.tə.bəl/

- very strong, especially in character; producing respect and a little fear in others
- causing or deserving great fear or respect : very powerful, impressive, etc.

Similar words: [[Formidable]], [[Distinguished]]

Mnemonic: Re + doubtable, there is no doubt, there are formidable.

Example: Tonight he faces the most redoubtable opponent of his boxing career.
