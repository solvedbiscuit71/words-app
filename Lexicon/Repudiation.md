# Repudiation

#hard 

rɪˌpjuː.diˈeɪ.ʃən

- the act of refusing to accept something or someone as true, good, or reasonable

Similar words: [[Denial]], [[Refusal]]

Mnemonic: Repudiation, refusing to accept someone's reputation.

Example: They were surprised by his sudden repudiation of all his former beliefs.
