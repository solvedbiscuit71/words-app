# Retiring

rɪˈtaɪə.rɪŋ

- unwilling to be noticed or to be with other people : shy
- used to refer to someone who is planning to leave their job and usually to stop working permanently because of age

Similar words: [[Shy]], [[Diffident]], [[Withdraw]]

Mnemonic: Someone who's retiring from networking events.

Example: He’s a shy, retiring sort of person.
