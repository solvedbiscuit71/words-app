# Schism

#hard 

/ˈskɪ.zəm/

- a division into two groups caused by a disagreement about ideas, especially in a religious organization
- a division of a group into two opposing groups, esp. in a church

Similar words: [[Discord]], [[Split]]

Mnemonic: Prism splits light, schism splits people

Example: The plan to appoint women as bishops caused a schism in/within the church.
