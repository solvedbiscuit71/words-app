# Sordid

/ˈsɔː.dɪd/

- dirty and unpleasant
- morally wrong and shocking
- morally ugly, so that being involved makes you feel dirty

Similar words: [[Squalid]], [[Filthy]]

Mnemonic: Sour + deed, something that's filthy and dirty

Example: The pernicious transformation of sordid stories into epics for profit.
