# Squalid

/ˈskwɒl.ɪd/

- (of places) extremely dirty and unpleasant, often because of lack of money
- (of situations and activities) not moral; involving sex and drugs, etc. in an unpleasant way
- extremely dirty, poor, and unpleasant

Similar words: [[Filthy]], [[Vile]]

Mnemonic: Squashed the lid, now the container is filthy

Example: Many prisons, even today, are overcrowded and squalid places.
