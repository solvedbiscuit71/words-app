# Temperate

tem·​per·​ate

- having temperatures that are not too hot or too cold
- emotionally calm and controlled
- avoiding behavior that goes beyond what is normal, healthy, or acceptable

Similar words: [[Temperance]], [[Ascetic]]

Near antonyms: [[Extreme]], [[Severe]], [[Voracious]]

Mnemonic: Temperate, temperance, similar words, similar meaning. But not temperament.
