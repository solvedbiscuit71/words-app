# Tenable

#hard 

/ˈten.ə.bəl/

- (of an opinion or position) able to be defended successfully or held for a particular period of time
- reasonable, or possible to achieve or defend

Similar words: [[Defendable]], [[Justifiable]]

Mnemonic: We have ten people to defend it, so it's defendable

Example: The fellowship is tenable for three years.
