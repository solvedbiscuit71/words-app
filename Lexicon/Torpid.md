# Torpid

#hard 

/ˈtɔː.pɪd/

- not active; moving or thinking slowly, especially as a result of being lazy or feeling that you want to sleep

Similar words: [[Sleepy]], [[Numb]]

Mnemonic: Tortoise, are torpid, they are slow and sleepy.

Example: a torpid sloth that refused to budge off its tree branch.
