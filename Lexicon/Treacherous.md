# Treacherous

/ˈtretʃ.ər.əs/

- (of the ground or the sea) extremely dangerous, esp. because of bad weather conditions
- (of a person) guilty of deceiving someone who trusts you

Similar words: [[Traitorous]], [[Unreliable]]

Mnemonic: When there is treasure involved, people become treacherous.

Example: He plays the part of a treacherous aristocrat who betrays his king and country.
