# Trepidation

ˌtre-pə-ˈdā-shən

-  a nervous or fearful feeling of uncertain agitation : apprehension
- a feeling of fear that causes you to hesitate because you think something bad or unpleasant is going to happen
- a tremulous motion : tremor

Similar words: [[Fear]], [[Apprehensive]]

Mnemonic: When there are traps set, you feel trepidation, you don't make a move
