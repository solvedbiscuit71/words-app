# Unwarranted

#hard 

ʌnˈwɒr.ən.tɪd

- not having a good reason and therefore annoying or unfair
- lacking a good reason; unnecessary

Similar words: [[Unnecessary]], [[Unreasonable]] 

Mnemonic: Think of, arrest warrant, reasonable evidence is needed, so unwarranted means unreasonable.

Example: They denounced the investigation as an unwarranted interference with their business.
