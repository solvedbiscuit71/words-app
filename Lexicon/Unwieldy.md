# Unwieldy

/ʌnˈwiːl.di/

- (of an object) difficult to move or handle because it is heavy, large, or a strange shape
- (of a system) difficult to manage, usually because it is too big or badly organized

Similar words: [[Clumsy]], [[Cumbersome]]

Mnemonic: Un + wield (to hold), unable to hold.

Example: A piano is a very unwieldy item to get down a flight of stairs.
