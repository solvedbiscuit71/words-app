# Vanity

væn.ə.ti

- the fact that you are too interested in your appearance or achievements
- used to describe something that is done with the aim of getting praise, fame, or approval rather than for serious or good reasons
- dressing table or a bathroom cabinet that is covered by a sink and a countertop

Similar words: [[Ego]]

Mnemonic: When someone has vanity, they will make you lose your sanity.

Example: He wants the job purely for reasons of vanity and ambition.
