# Waiver

/ˈweɪ.vər/

- an agreement that you do not have to pay or obey something
- the act of a sports team removing a player from its roster and making that player available to other teams

Similar words: [[Exemption]]

Mnemonic: Please sign the waiver

Example: Before the hospital will treat you, you have to sign a waiver in which you give up your right to sue.
